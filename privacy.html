Privacy

Updated October 19, 2026. Short version: your footage and tracks are processed on your device and we never receive them.

On your device

Videos you add, track files you import, and the videos the app renders are handled only on your iPhone or iPad. The app reads a video's recording time and location metadata to sync it with the tracks; that stays on the device. Your overlay settings are stored on the device.

Map tiles

To draw the fleet map the app downloads map images for the area your tracks cover from Esri (World Light Gray Canvas) and OpenSeaMap. Those requests include the tile coordinates, which reveal the general area, plus your IP address as with any web request. No identifier of yours is sent.

Optional: WakeRoute results page

If you tap "Make a replay page on wakeroute.com", the GPS tracks you imported (never videos) and an optional title are uploaded to WakeRoute, a race-results service run by the same developer, to create a public results page reachable by its link. This happens only when you choose it. See wakeroute.com/privacy for how that service stores data.

Sharing

When you save to Photos, share, or send to an Instagram Story, the video goes where you send it, under that app's terms.

What we don't do

No account, no analytics or advertising SDKs, no tracking across apps, no sale of data.
